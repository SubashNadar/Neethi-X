# 🏛️ LEGALCHAT - Pitch Deck

## The Problem
- **90% of Indians can't afford legal advice** (₹3000-10000 per consultation)
- 70% face legal issues annually but have **zero access**
- Current solutions: Expensive, slow (2-3 day wait), not 24/7

## The Solution
**AI-First Legal Assistant** for the masses
- **Free AI chat** powered by Claude + Indian Penal Code
- **Affordable lawyers** when needed (₹500 initial vs ₹5000 typical)
- **Instant response** + scalable (1 lawyer can help 100s via AI)

## The Market
```
Total Addressable Market (TAM):
1.4 Billion Indians × 70% with legal issues × ₹1000/year = ₹980,000 Crore

Conservative: 0.1% share = ₹196 Crore market
Our Target Year 1: ₹2 Crore+ revenue (100K DAU)
```

## Revenue Model
```
Free → Paid Funnel

User Flow:
1. Free AI chat (asks legal question)
2. AI analyzes + provides guidance
3. AI suggests "Talk to lawyer?" 
4. User pays ₹500-2000 (we keep 30%)
5. Video call with verified lawyer
6. Optional: Monthly subscription (₹299)

Example Math:
- 100K users/month
- 10% book lawyer = 10K consultations
- ₹1000 avg × 30% = ₹300
- ₹300 × 10K = ₹30L/month profit ✅
```
